# About This Portfolio

I'm **Anand** — a technical writer with 10 years of experience across banking, semiconductors, enterprise software, SaaS, and procurement.

Most of my career has been spent working on complex, high-stakes documentation: products where a missing step or an ambiguous sentence causes real problems — a failed transaction, a misconfigured chip, a broken integration.

---

## What I Do Well

**Doc architecture.** I don't just write — I figure out what needs to exist, how it should be structured, and how users will navigate it. I've built documentation sets from scratch and inherited ones that needed untangling.

**Simplifying complex concepts.** Whether it's explaining a semiconductor's power management subsystem or how OAuth 2.0 actually works under the hood, I focus on making the underlying logic clear — not just listing steps.

**DITA and structured authoring.** I work natively in DITA using Oxygen XML Author and Ixiasoft CCMS, with Git for version control. I'm comfortable in the full docs-as-code workflow: authoring, review, and automated publishing.
