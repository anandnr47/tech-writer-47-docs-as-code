# About This Portfolio

I'm **Anand**, a technical writer with 10 years of experience across banking, semiconductors, enterprise software, SaaS, and procurement.

Most of my career has been on documentation where accuracy matters. A wrong step in a financial workflow causes a failed transaction. A missing prerequisite in a hardware setup bricks a device. I've spent a lot of time writing for users who can't afford to guess.

---

## What I Do Well

**Doc architecture.** I don't just write. I figure out what needs to exist, how it should be structured, and how users will find what they need. I've built documentation sets from scratch and inherited ones that needed untangling.

**Simplifying complex concepts.** Whether it's a semiconductor's power management subsystem or how OAuth 2.0 works under the hood, I focus on making the logic clear. Not just listing steps.

**DITA and structured authoring.** I work natively in DITA using Oxygen XML Author and Ixiasoft CCMS, with Git for version control. I'm comfortable in the full docs-as-code workflow: authoring, review, and automated publishing.
